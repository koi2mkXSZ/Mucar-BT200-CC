# BT200 transport evidence — 2026-09-27 Delphi session

Source: decoded frame capture `9TFD20111779_QIRUI_TY_20260927142439.frames.csv`.

This document records observed facts only. The outer checksum/framing algorithm and Android Bluetooth bearer are not yet claimed.

## Adapter command envelope observed

After the initial application/license traffic, diagnostic-adapter commands repeatedly use:

`SEQ 27 01 ...` for adapter/control requests and `SEQ 67 01 ...` for their responses.

Examples:

- `02 27 01 60 21` -> `02 67 01 00 07`
- `09 27 01 60 27` -> `09 67 01 00 02`
- `27 27 01 ... 61 09 ... 81 11 F1 81 04`
  -> `27 67 01 00 01 07 83 F1 11 C1 EF 8F C4`

The first byte increments as a transaction sequence value and the response echoes it.

## Confirmed KWP pass-through

Transaction 0x27 contains the known StartCommunication frame:

`81 11 F1 81 04`

Response contains:

`83 F1 11 C1 EF 8F C4`

Transaction 0x40 contains the known ReadDataByLocalIdentifier request:

`80 11 F1 02 21 01 A6`

Response begins:

`40 67 01 00 01 53 80 F1 11 4E 61 01 ...`

Therefore the capture proves that Launch/MUCAR adapter traffic encapsulates complete KWP frames; the ECU protocol does not need to be translated to ELM327 AT commands.

## Important boundary

The decoded DiagnoseLog is sufficient to reconstruct the DPU transaction sequence, but it does **not** by itself prove whether the phone-to-BT200 bearer is Bluetooth Classic RFCOMM or BLE/GATT, nor the UUIDs/characteristics. That requires the DollarFix/MUCAR APK or an Android Bluetooth capture.
